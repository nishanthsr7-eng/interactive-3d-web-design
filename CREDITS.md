# Credits

## 3D model

The hero wireframe is derived from
[Make your own steampunk house](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/make-your-own-steampunk-house-0bceb4f2d7444809b022ba864ac03df3)
by [Conrad Justin](https://sketchfab.com/ConradJustin), used under the
[Sketchfab Standard licence](https://sketchfab.com/licenses).

The original 116 MB model is not in this repository and is never loaded in the
browser. `scripts/build-model-wire.mjs` extracts only its feature edges, and
those edges are what ship in `public/assets/model/`. The same credit is recorded
in `public/assets/model/house-wire.json`, copied from the model file's own
metadata.
